# Apocalyptic Dominion - Maximally Expanded

**The definitive post-apocalyptic Unciv mod.**

A massively expanded base ruleset mod featuring deep adult-themed mechanics (domination, slavery, breeding programs, harem management, and moral choice between tyranny and survival) alongside extensive traditional 4X content.

## Current Features

### Civilizations
- **Wasteland Dominion** (Overlord Vex / Matriarch Lira)
- **Iron Claws** (Warlord Karg)
- **Breeder Covenant** (Matriarch Lira)
- **Iron Reavers** (Warlord Thorne)

### Units
- 12+ custom units including Raiders, Enforcers, Slave Laborers, Slave Overseers, Mobile Fortresses, Drone Swarms, and Legendary units.

### Buildings
- Thematic buildings including Slave Pens, Breeding Facilities, Raider Camps, Pleasure Domes, Enslavement Chambers, Grand Harems, Central Breeding Complexes, and more.

### Technologies
- Full thematic tech tree with paths for Raiding, Domination, Breeding, and Reclamation.

### Policies & Promotions
- Custom policies with tyranny, breeding, and raiding synergies.
- Branching unit promotion lines for Raider, Enforcer, and Laborer paths.

### Tile Improvements
- Raider Camp, Labor Outpost, Breeding Pit, Slave Quarry, Enforcer Watchtower, Reclamation Zone.

### Global Effects
- Mod-wide uniques supporting war production, slave management, and defensive city building.

## Adult-Themed Mechanics (Abstracted)
All mature themes are fully abstracted into strategic systems:
- Population growth and breeding efficiency as growth multipliers
- Captured enemies converted into productive Slave Laborers
- Happiness vs. power trade-offs through tyranny and domination policies
- Harem and slave management buildings/policies

## Installation
1. In Unciv go to **Mods** → **Download mod from URL**
2. Paste: `https://github.com/01talib18-web/unciv-apocalyptic-dominion-mod`
3. Download and enable the mod

## Compatibility
- Base ruleset mod (can be used alone or combined carefully with other mods)
- Designed for stability on Android and Desktop
